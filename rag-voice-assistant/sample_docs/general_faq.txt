Q: How can I register to vote?
A: Visit your nearest voter registration center or apply online at voters.eci.gov.in with your Aadhaar card.

Q: What is the party's stance on unemployment?
A: Jana Seva Party promises to create 2 lakh jobs in Andhra Pradesh within 2 years through IT parks and manufacturing zones.

Q: Are there schemes for women?
A: Yes. YSR Cheyutha provides Rs 18,750 annually to women from BC, SC, ST, and minority communities aged 45-60.
