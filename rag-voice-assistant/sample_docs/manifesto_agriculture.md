# Jana Seva Party — Agriculture Manifesto

## Vision
Transform Andhra Pradesh into India's leading agricultural state by 2030.

## Key Policies

### Water & Irrigation
- Restore and expand Krishna and Godavari canal networks across 18 districts.
- Solar-powered micro-irrigation for 5 lakh small farmers by 2025.
- Free borewells for dry-land farmers in Rayalaseema region.

### Financial Support
- Double the YSR Rythu Bharosa assistance from Rs 13,500 to Rs 27,000 per acre.
- Zero-interest crop loans up to Rs 1 lakh for marginal farmers.
- Crop loss compensation within 72 hours of natural disaster.

### Technology
- AI-powered soil health cards for every farm in Andhra Pradesh.
- Direct market linkage app connecting farmers to buyers, eliminating middlemen.
