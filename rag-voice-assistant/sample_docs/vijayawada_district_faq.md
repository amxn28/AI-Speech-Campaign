# Vijayawada — Voter FAQ

## General
Q: Which constituency does Vijayawada fall under?
A: Vijayawada is divided into Vijayawada East, Vijayawada West, and Vijayawada Central constituencies.

## Candidate
Q: Who is the Jana Seva Party candidate for Vijayawada East?
A: Ravi Kumar Reddy, former district collector with 15 years of administrative experience.

## Schemes
Q: What schemes are specifically available for Vijayawada residents?
A: Vijayawada residents can avail YSR Rythu Bharosa, YSR Cheyutha, free healthcare under Aarogyasri, and subsidized housing under YSR Housing scheme.
